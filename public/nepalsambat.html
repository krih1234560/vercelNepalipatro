Nepal Sambat Calendar FAQ

What is Nepal Sambat?

Nepal Sambat is the traditional calendar associated with Nepal and the Newar community. This page provides an interactive calendar view and date-conversion tools.

Can I convert Nepal Sambat dates?

Yes. Use the converter above to view the corresponding Bikram Sambat and Gregorian dates where supported by this calendar.
