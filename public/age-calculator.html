About This Age Calculator

Use this free online age calculator to find your age in years, months and days. You can enter dates in the Gregorian AD calendar or in Nepal's commonly used BS (Bikram Sambat) calendar. For BS input, the calculator converts the date to AD internally before calculating the difference between the birth date and the selected “as of” date.

How to Calculate Your Age

  1. Select AD (English) or BS (Nepali).
  2. Enter your date of birth.
  3. Choose the date you want to calculate your age as of.
  4. Click Calculate Age to see the result in years, months and days.

AD vs BS Age Calculation

AD refers to the Gregorian calendar, while BS refers to Bikram Sambat, the calendar system widely used for dates in Nepal. If you have a Nepali birth date but need your age based on a Gregorian date, use the BS option here rather than manually estimating the year difference.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate age using a Nepali BS date?

Yes. Select BS (Nepali), enter the birth date and the “as of” date in BS year, month and day, then calculate.

Does the calculator show years, months and days?

Yes. The result is displayed as a combination of completed years, months and days between the selected dates.

Can I calculate age as of a past or future date?

Yes, as long as the dates are valid and the birth date is not later than the calculation date.
