What is Rashifal (राशिफल)?

Rashifal is an integral part of Nepali culture and daily life. Many people in Nepal consult their rashifal before starting important work, making decisions, or planning events. The predictions are based on the position of planets and stars at the time of birth, and are calculated using the Bikram Sambat calendar which is the official calendar of Nepal.

Why Rashifal is Important in Nepali Culture

In Nepal, rashifal (राशिफल) is consulted for various life events including: marriage (विवाह), business ventures (व्यापार), travel (यात्रा), education (शिक्षा), and health decisions. Many Nepalis start their day by reading the daily rashifal to get guidance and understand what to expect. About Bikram Sambat Calendar

The Bikram Sambat (BS) calendar is the traditional Hindu calendar used in Nepal. It is approximately 56.7 years ahead of the Gregorian calendar. The year 2083 BS corresponds to 2026-2027 AD. This Rashifal tool allows you to select any BS date from 1975 to 2100 to get accurate horoscope predictions based on the Nepali calendar system.
