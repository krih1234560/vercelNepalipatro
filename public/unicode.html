Combine English with Nepali Unicode

Need to keep an English word inside your Nepali sentence? Place the English text inside curly brackets.

Type: yo {mobile} mero ho.

Result: यो mobile मेरो हो।

Use / to Separate Letters

If a Nepali letter is incorrectly combined with the preceding letter, use the slash key to separate them.

Without separator pratishatko प्रतिशत्को
With separator pratishat/ko प्रतिशतको
्

Add Halant at the End of a Word

To add a हलन्त at the end of a Nepali word, use the backslash key.

Normal bas बस
With Halant bas\ बस्
