What is Kundli Milan?

Kundli Milan, also called Guna Milan or Ashtakoot matching, is a traditional Vedic astrology method used to compare two birth charts for marriage compatibility. This tool is designed to make the comparison process simple: enter both people's birth details and review the eight-koota, 36-point compatibility breakdown.

For a more complete chart, use our Janma Kundali generator. You can also use the BS–AD date converter when birth records use different calendar systems.

Frequently Asked Questions About Kundli Milan

What is Kundli Milan?

Kundli Milan compares two birth charts using the traditional Ashtakoot or Guna Milan framework. It is commonly used as one factor when families evaluate marriage compatibility.

How many points are there in Guna Milan?

The eight kootas together have a maximum of 36 points: Varna 1, Vashya 2, Tara 3, Yoni 4, Graha Maitri 5, Gana 6, Bhakoot 7 and Nadi 8.

Is 18 out of 36 a good match?

An 18-point threshold is commonly referenced in traditional Guna Milan, but the total alone is not a complete compatibility judgment. Individual kootas and the full birth charts may need separate interpretation.

What details do I need to match two Kundlis?

You need each person's date of birth, birth time and place of birth. Names are used by this interface for identification of the result.

Can I use Nepali Bikram Sambat birth dates?

If a birth record is in Bikram Sambat, convert it to the calendar format expected by your astrology workflow using the BS–AD date converter before entering the details.
